This website provides information about and links to the *Laurence Salzmann and Ayşe Gürsan-Salzmann Collection*. Donated by Laurence and Ayşe Gürsan-Salzmann in 2018, the Salzmann Collection gift comprises more than twenty distinct photographic projects by the Philadelphia-born photographer, filmmaker, and book publisher, Laurence Salzmann. This extraordinary visual body of work, amounting to almost 2,700 images, spans more than a half-century of his career from 1967–2018 and traverses four continents. His interdisciplinary projects cover a range of human experiences, documenting indigenous people in Mexico, Cuba, and Peru, the last surviving members of Jewish communities in Romania and Turkey, Black-Jewish relationships in the United States, life in Single Room Occupancy hotels in New York City during the 1960s, and lived experiences on the streets of Philadelphia, as well as a range of abstract works of photography.
